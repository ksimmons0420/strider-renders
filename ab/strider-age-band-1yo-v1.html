Parent questions

One-year-old, two wheels?

Is my 1-year-old too young?

Not at all. The 2-in-1 Rocking Bike is designed for riders as young as 6 months. The rocking base lets babies build confidence and balance safely — long before they can walk.

Why a balance bike instead of a tricycle or training wheels?

Tricycles and training wheels teach pedaling but let kids lean on stability that isn’t really there. A balance bike teaches the actual skill — balance — first. Most kids then move straight to a pedal bike with no training wheels at all.

Rocking Bike or 12 Sport — which one?

For a 1-year-old, start with the 2-in-1 Rocking Bike ($299.98) — it rocks now and detaches into a balance bike when they walk. If your child is already walking confidently, the 12 Sport ($169.99) is a great place to begin.

Years. Tool-free seat and handlebar adjustments mean the bike grows from toddler to preschooler. When they’re ready for pedals, the convertible 14x lets you add a pedal kit to the same style of bike — no need to start over.

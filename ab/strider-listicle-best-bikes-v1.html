Common questions

Balance bike questions, answered

What age should a child start a balance bike?

As early as 6 months with the 2-in-1 Rocking Bike, and around 18 months for a standard 12-inch balance bike. If your child can walk, they can start learning to balance.

Are balance bikes better than training wheels?

Yes — balance bikes teach the actual skill (balancing) first, so most kids move straight to a pedal bike with no training wheels at all.

Which Strider is the lightest?

The 12 Comp at 4.9 lbs (composite frame), followed by the 12 Pro at 5.6 lbs (aluminum).

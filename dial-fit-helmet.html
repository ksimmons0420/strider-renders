Good questions

Before you buy

How is a dial-fit helmet different from a normal one?
Instead of a buckle, the Dial-Fit Helmet uses exclusive HighBar® technology: a soft chinstrap you swing down and a rear dial you turn to adjust. There's nothing to clip, pinch, or tangle — so kids can put it on and take it off themselves.
Can my child really put it on by themselves?
Yes — that's the whole idea. You set the dial once for their head, and from then on they simply pull the strap down and give the dial a few turns. Many kids can do it one-handed.
What size is it and who does it fit?
The Dial-Fit Helmet comes in one size (Size Small), fitting head circumferences of 51–54 cm (20–21.25 in). Kids' heads vary a lot at every age, so we go by measurement rather than age — measure around the widest part of your child's head, just above the eyebrows, and the dial fine-tunes the rest.
Is it safe / certified?
A properly fitted helmet is far safer — and the Dial-Fit Helmet is designed to give a reliable, snug fit every ride, because a helmet only protects when it fits right. [Certification wording — pending Alyssa]
What can they wear it for?
Biking, scooting, skateboarding, or riding along as a passenger — it's built to protect them across all their wheels.

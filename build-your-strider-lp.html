Build it for how they ride

Three choices that make it theirs

Every part is engineered and sized for a small rider from the ground up — not a shrunk-down adult bike or a big-box light-up toy. Here’s how to dial the build in for your kid.

Frame: Sport or Pro

The 12 Sport is the iconic steel build — durable, everyday-ready, and a proven starter for ages 1–4. The 12 Pro is premium aluminum and a full pound lighter (5.6 lbs) — easy for the tiniest or most timid riders to pick up and steer on their own, and a favorite of fast, competitive kids.

Wheels: Ultralight or High-Traction

Ultralight foam wheels are the beginner default — feather-light, maintenance-free, and they never go flat, so a brand-new rider can just ride (indoors or out). High-Traction wheels are the upgrade once they’re around 3 and riding faster — air-filled for superior grip and extra shock absorption on rougher, quicker terrain.

Handlebar & grips

Pick the standard handlebar for comfortable everyday riding, or a flat bar to set a confident rider in a lower, race-ready stance — then finish with the grip and frame colors that make it unmistakably theirs.

Good questions

Before you build

Which Strider is right for my child’s age?
Most kids start on a 12-inch Strider somewhere between around 18 months and 5 years, and the adjustable seat and bars keep it fitting as they grow. And if it isn’t the right fit, you’re covered by our 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee.
Sport or Pro — what’s the difference?
The 12 Sport is the original, most-popular first Strider — durable, adjustable, and ready to ride. The 12 Pro steps up to ultralight aluminum with more adjustability and premium components. Both use the same balance-first design; both fully customize in the builder.
Does it really “grow with them”?
Yes — the tool-free adjustable seat and handlebars raise as your child does, so one bike carries them through the balance years. And when they’re ready for a bigger bike, the convertible 14x lets pedals add to the same familiar ride.
Why skip training wheels?
Balance bikes teach the hardest part first — balancing — so the move to a pedal bike tends to be faster and more confident, with no training-wheel habits to unlearn. It’s the approach that helped over 10 million kids learn to ride.
How much does a built bike cost?
The 12 Sport starts at $104.98 and the 12 Pro at $144.98 before options. As you choose colors, wheels and grips, the builder updates your exact price live — so what you see is what you pay.
What about shipping and returns?
Strider bikes ship free*, and every one is backed by a 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee. Build it, try it at home, and return it if it’s not right.
